# AuraLoop Electronics - Policies & FAQ

AuraLoop Electronics is an online retailer of headphones, smartwatches, home
speakers, cameras, and computer accessories, shipping across the United States.

## Return & Refund Policy

### Return window
- Items may be returned for a full refund within **30 days of delivery**.
- The 30-day window is measured from the delivery date shown on the order,
  not the order/purchase date.
- Returns requested on day 30 are accepted; requests from day 31 onward are
  outside the window and are not eligible for a standard refund.

### Eligible order states
- Refunds can only be processed for orders that have a status of **delivered**
  or **delivered - refund requested**.
- Orders that are still **processing** or **in transit** are not yet eligible
  for a refund because they have not been delivered - customers should wait
  for delivery or request a cancellation instead (see Cancellations below).
- Orders already marked **refunded** cannot be refunded again.
- Orders that were **cancelled** before shipment are not eligible for a refund
  because no payment capture/refund cycle applies to them under this policy.

### Refund amount and threshold review
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refunds for orders with a total value of **$200.00 or less** are processed
  automatically once eligibility is confirmed.
- Refunds for orders **over $200.00** always require review by a member of
  the support team before being issued, regardless of how clearly the order
  meets the return-window and status criteria above.
- If an order's delivery date is missing or its status doesn't clearly match
  one of the eligible states, the request is treated as ambiguous and is
  routed to a human agent rather than auto-approved or auto-denied.

### Condition of returned items
- Items should be returned unused, in their original packaging, with all
  accessories and manuals included, to qualify for a full refund.
- Items returned used, damaged (beyond normal inspection), or missing
  accessories may receive a partial refund at the support team's discretion.

### Non-returnable items
- Gift cards are final sale and not eligible for return or refund.
- Earbuds and other in-ear audio products that have been opened cannot be
  returned for hygiene reasons unless they are defective.

### Cancellations
- Orders that are still in **processing** status (not yet shipped) can be
  cancelled for a full refund upon request.
- Once an order moves to **in transit**, it can no longer be cancelled - the
  customer should wait for delivery and then request a standard return.

### Defective or incorrect items
- If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered,
  the customer is entitled to a full refund or a free replacement regardless
  of the 30-day window, provided it is reported within 45 days of delivery.
- These reports should always be flagged to a human agent to verify the
  defect claim and arrange replacement shipping.

## Shipping Policy

### Shipping methods and timelines
- **Standard shipping** (free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99): 5-7
  business days.
- **Expedited shipping** ($14.99): 2-3 business days.
- **Overnight shipping** ($29.99): next business day if ordered before 2 PM
  local time on a business day.

### Order processing time
- Orders are processed (picked, packed, and handed to the carrier) within
  1-2 business days of being placed, before the shipping timeline above
  begins.

### Tracking
- A tracking number is generated once an order leaves the **processing**
  status and moves to **in transit**. Orders still in processing do not yet
  have a tracking number.
- Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to first appear after a tracking
  number is issued.

### Shipping destinations
- AuraLoop currently ships only within the United States, including Alaska
  and Hawaii (an additional 2-3 business days should be expected for these
  two states).
- AuraLoop does not currently ship to PO boxes for orders containing
  cameras or smartwatches, due to carrier insurance requirements.

### Lost or delayed packages
- If a package shows no tracking movement for more than 7 business days,
  the customer should contact support so a trace can be filed with the
  carrier - this should be routed to a human agent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

1. **How do I check my order status?**
   Provide your order number (format `ORD-XXXXXX`) and we can look up the
   current status, tracking number, and expected delivery window.

2. **Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
   Only while the order is still in **processing** status. Once it moves to
   **in transit**, the address cannot be changed and a human agent should be
   contacted to explore options with the carrier.

3. **Do you offer international shipping?**
   Not currently - AuraLoop ships only within the United States.

4. **What payment methods are accepted?**
   Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal.

5. **How long does a refund take to appear once approved?**
   Approved refunds are issued immediately in our system and typically
   appear on the original payment method within 5-10 business days,
   depending on the card issuer or bank.

6. **Can I return part of a multi-item order?**
   Yes - individual items within an order can be returned; the refund
   amount is calculated based on that item's price, and the $200 review
   threshold is evaluated against the full order total, not the single
   item's price.

7. **What is AuraLoop's warranty policy?**
   All products carry a 1-year manufacturer's warranty against defects.
   Warranty claims after the 30-day return window should be routed to a
   human agent to verify the manufacturer's process.

8. **Do you price-match?**
   AuraLoop does not currently offer price matching.

9. **How do I know if an item is in stock?**
   Ask about a specific product by name or category and we can check
   current stock levels in the catalog.

10. **I received the wrong item - what do I do?**
    This is treated as a defective/incorrect item case (see Return & Refund
    Policy above) and is always routed to a human agent to arrange a
    replacement or refund outside the standard flow.

11. **Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?**
    No - once an order is **in transit** it cannot be cancelled, only
    returned after delivery under the standard return policy.

12. **Is there a restocking fee?**
    No restocking fees are charged on eligible returns.

13. **Do gift cards expire?**
    No, AuraLoop gift cards do not expire, but they are final sale and
    non-refundable.

14. **How do I redeem a discount code?**
    Discount codes are applied at checkout on the AuraLoop website; codes
    cannot be applied retroactively to an order that has already been
    placed.

15. **What happens if my refund request is for an order over $200?**
    It is automatically routed to a human support agent for review, even
    if the order is clearly within the 30-day window and delivered - this
    is a fixed policy threshold, not a judgment call made per-case.

16. **Can I return an item bought as a gift?**
    Yes, as long as the order number or a receipt is provided; refunds are
    issued to the original payment method used for the order, not to the
    gift recipient.

17. **My tracking number isn't working on the carrier's site yet.**
    This is normal for up to 24 hours after an order moves to in transit.
    If it still doesn't work after 24 hours, contact support.

18. **Do you accept returns without the original packaging?**
    Returns are accepted without original packaging but may receive a
    reduced (partial) refund at the support team's discretion.

19. **How many items can I order at once?**
    There is a limit of 10 units per product per order to ensure fair
    availability during high-demand periods.

20. **Who do I contact for a business/bulk order inquiry?**
    Bulk and business orders are not handled through this support channel
    and should always be escalated to a human agent.
