# Smile Care Dental Clinics

Your Smile, Our Priority

Smile Care Dental Clinics has been serving the community with compassionate,
high-quality dental care for over a decade. Our team of experienced dentists and
specialists is committed to helping every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile
using modern equipment and patient-friendly techniques.

## Services & Pricing

Below is a general price list for our most commonly requested treatments. Final costs
may vary depending on individual case complexity and will be confirmed during
consultation.

- **Dental Check-up & Consultation** - Comprehensive oral examination with diagnosis
  and treatment planning - $45
- **Teeth Cleaning (Scaling & Polishing)** - Removal of plaque and tartar with
  professional polishing - $70
- **Tooth Filling (Composite)** - Tooth-colored filling for cavities, per tooth - $85
- **Root Canal Treatment** - Endodontic treatment to save an infected tooth, per
  tooth - $350
- **Tooth Extraction** - Simple extraction of a damaged or decayed tooth - $60
- **Teeth Whitening** - In-office professional whitening treatment - $220
- **Dental Crown (Ceramic)** - Custom-fitted crown to restore a damaged tooth - $450
- **Dental Implants** - Titanium implant with abutment, per tooth (crown separate) -
  $1,200
- **Braces / Orthodontic Treatment** - Metal braces including consultation, full
  course - $2,800
- **Pediatric Dental Care** - Check-up and preventive care for children - $40
- **Dentures (Full Set)** - Custom-fitted upper and lower dentures - $900
- **Emergency Dental Visit** - Urgent care for pain, trauma, or infection - $95
